Designed for deliberate practice.

CardioSim is a reusable anatomical silicone heart for cardiovascular suturing practice. It gives doctors, surgeons and students a model to rehearse stitching technique on, as often as they choose.

Anatomical detail
Anatomical folds and contours modelled in three dimensions, in deep red silicone with one white central vessel.
Hands-on suturing practice
A physical model to hold, turn and stitch, so technique is rehearsed by hand rather than on a screen.
Flexible training environments
Made for use outside the clinical environment: at home, in a university laboratory or in a training facility.

Questions

Who is CardioSim designed for?

Doctors, surgeons, medical students and cardiovascular specialists who want to rehearse suturing and stitching technique on a physical, three-dimensional heart model.

Can CardioSim be used for home practice?

Yes. CardioSim is a training model for use outside the clinical environment: at home, in a university laboratory or in a medical training facility.

Which surgical techniques can be practiced?

CardioSim is made for suturing and stitching practice. The list of specific techniques and the practice areas they belong to is being confirmed by the product team. Until then, the points shown in the 3D explorer are demonstration points.

Can the product be reused?

CardioSim is intended as a reusable model. How many practice sessions it withstands has not been confirmed yet and will be published with the final specifications.

What is included with an order?

The CardioSim Anatomical Silicone Heart. Any further components are still to be confirmed and will be listed in the specifications.

How can I contact CardioSim?

The support address is being set up and will be published on the contact page.
