Made for practice.

CardioSim is a silicone heart model for practising suturing and stitching technique: a red body, one large white vessel opening at the top and a white line down the front.

A training object
CardioSim is a simplified, heart-shaped model with a moulded surface. It is made for practising technique, not as an anatomical replica.
Hands-on practice
A physical model to hold, turn and stitch, so technique is rehearsed by hand rather than on a screen.
Outside the operating room
For doctors, medical students and healthcare professionals, at home or in a training setting.

Questions

Who is CardioSim designed for?

Doctors, medical students and healthcare professionals who want to practise heart suturing and stitching technique on a physical model.

Can it be used at home?

Yes. CardioSim is made for practice outside the operating room, at home or in a training setting.

What kind of stitching practice is it intended for?

Hands-on suturing and stitching practice on a silicone heart model. The areas shown on this site are illustrative: a list of specific techniques will be published once it is confirmed.

Is it an anatomical replica?

No. CardioSim is a simplified, heart-shaped training model. It is built for practising technique, not as a museum-grade anatomical reproduction.

Is it reusable?

How many practice sessions one model withstands has not been confirmed yet. The answer will be published with the final product details.
